====================================================================
                  PANELPOWER - EASY 3-STEP SETUP
        Mac App: v1.1.2  |  Hubitat Driver: v2.2.2 (2026-11-13)
====================================================================

What's in this folder:
  1. PanelPower.app                          -> The Mac Menu Bar App (v1.1.2)
  2. Tuya_Zigbee_Metering_Plug_v2.2.2.groovy -> The Hubitat Driver (v2.2.2)
  3. HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt                      -> This guide


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL THE MAC APP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Drag "PanelPower.app" into your Mac's "Applications" folder.
2. Open your Applications folder, RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) on
   "PanelPower.app" and choose "Open".
   (Note: Because this is a free community app and not from the Mac
   App Store, macOS may ask you to confirm opening it the first time.
   If macOS Sequoia blocks it, go to System Settings -> Privacy &
   Security -> scroll down and click "Open Anyway".)
3. Look at the top-right Menu Bar on your Mac screen — you will now
   see a lightning bolt icon (⚡) with live demo numbers!


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: INSTALL THE HUBITAT DRIVER (SKIP IF USING HOME ASSISTANT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
If your Zigbee CT Clamp meter is paired to a Hubitat Elevation hub:

1. Open your Hubitat web page in your browser.
2. In the left sidebar, click "Drivers Code" (under Developer Tools).
3. Click "+ New Driver" in the top right.
4. Click the "Import" button at the top of the screen, paste this
   link, and click Import:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drfiero-afaik/PowerPanel/main/Tuya_Zigbee_Metering_Plug.groovy
   (Or open "Tuya_Zigbee_Metering_Plug.groovy" from this folder in
   TextEdit, copy all the text, and paste it in.)
5. Click "Save" in the top right.
6. Go to "Devices" in Hubitat, click on your Zigbee Energy Meter,
   scroll to the "Type" dropdown, select "Tuya Zigbee Metering Plug",
   and click "Save Device".
7. Look at your browser's address bar while on that device's page —
   the number at the very end of the URL (for example, /device/edit/693)
   is your Device ID (e.g. 693). Write that number down!


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: CONNECT PANELPOWER TO YOUR HUB
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click the lightning bolt (⚡) in your Mac's top Menu Bar.
2. Click the small Gear icon (⚙️) in the bottom-right corner of the
   popup window.
3. Choose your setup:
   - For Hubitat:
     * Click "Hubitat (Direct)" at the top.
     * Enter your Hubitat IP address (example: http://192.168.1.50).
     * Enter the Device ID number from Step 2 (example: 693).
   - For Home Assistant:
     * Click "Home Assistant" at the top.
     * Enter your Home Assistant URL and Long-Lived Access Token, then
       click "Auto-Discover Sensors".
4. Click "Test Connection", then click "Done".


--------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL: START AUTOMATICALLY WHEN YOU LOG IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open macOS "System Settings" -> "General" -> "Login Items".
2. Click the "+" button under "Open at Login".
3. Select "PanelPower" from your Applications folder.
